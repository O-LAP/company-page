Prismo

Product Mockup Automation Software

Generate Product Mockups in Seconds

Transform textile artwork into professional, production-ready previews for apparel, home furnishings, merchandising, and sampling workflows.

How it works

1. Select a template

Choose from predefined templates representing various products. Load the template into your workspace to define the structure of your mockup.

2. Import and position artwork

Import your design files (JPG, PNG, TIFF). Position and manage multiple artworks and variants on the template to create complex compositions.

3. Fine-tune and transform

Use advanced features like scaling and labeling to make precise adjustments. Preview the final image to ensure it meets your production standards.

4. Export high-quality images

Generate final mockup images in high-resolution JPG or PNG format. Use the batch export settings to process multiple files quickly and consistently.
